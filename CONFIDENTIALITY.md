# Confidentiality & Evidence Standard

This is a **public professional portfolio**. Its purpose is to demonstrate operating judgement without publishing employer-owned material, production data or private information.

## Core rule

> Public career facts may be named. Operating evidence must be independently reconstructed, company-neutral and safe to publish.

## What may appear here

- Public employer names, role titles and employment dates
- Publicly safe career outcomes and metrics used in my professional positioning
- Independently written frameworks and case studies
- Synthetic examples and datasets
- Portfolio-safe diagrams and operating models
- High-level retrospectives for ventures I own or built

## What must not appear here

- Employer-owned documents, decks, spreadsheets, prompts or source code
- Production CRM, marketing, product, customer or financial data
- Customer, prospect, employee, partner or supplier lists
- Internal screenshots, dashboards, private URLs or system identifiers
- Credentials, API keys, tokens, recovery codes or environment files
- Contracts, compensation records, identity documents or personal records
- Exact proprietary configurations or unpublished commercial information

## Employer-related case studies

Case studies are reconstructed from professional experience rather than copied from historic source artefacts. They use neutral descriptions and remove identifying internal terminology, people, customer information and proprietary implementation detail.

Where a piece of work is synthetic, reconstructed, conceptual or still being tested, it is labelled accordingly.

## Metrics

Metrics are used only where they are part of the approved professional evidence base and safe to disclose. The portfolio does not invent outcomes to make a case study appear stronger.

## Independent builds

ThinkBud and LYNR are independent founder builds, not employers. They are included only to demonstrate how the same operating judgement transfers into product and service environments. Their private repositories, user data, credentials and commercially sensitive implementation details remain outside this portfolio.

## Public-use note

The repository is public for professional review. Public visibility does not transfer ownership of the original writing, frameworks or portfolio material. Third-party methods are attributed where relevant, and no third-party training material is presented as original intellectual property.
