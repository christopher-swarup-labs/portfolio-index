# Confidentiality Policy

## Purpose

This portfolio is a private, curated evidence room showing how Christopher Swarup approaches revenue systems, GTM operations, martech, analytics, AI workflows and venture building.

It is not a storage location for historic employer documents.

## Information classes

### 1. Portfolio-ready

Material created specifically for this portfolio using original wording, neutral terminology and synthetic examples.

Examples:

- Independently written operating frameworks
- Newly created diagrams
- Synthetic datasets
- Generic process models
- High-level venture case studies based on owned work

### 2. Rebuild before use

Source material that contains useful professional learning but cannot be copied into GitHub.

Examples:

- Historic operating models
- Internal process presentations
- CRM field definitions
- Governance manuals
- Dashboards and reports
- Transformation plans

The underlying idea may be extracted, but the portfolio version must be rebuilt from scratch.

### 3. Restricted source

Material that may inform private analysis but must never appear in a repository.

Examples:

- Internal company data
- Employee or customer information
- Screenshots from production systems
- Contracts, pricing or commercial terms
- Security designs
- Internal URLs or system identifiers
- Exact proprietary configuration

### 4. Never include

- Passwords, API keys, tokens or credentials
- Recovery codes or backup codes
- Identity documents
- Payroll, tax or banking records
- Personal family information
- Customer lists or contact exports
- Signed contracts or legal documents belonging to a third party

## Employer anonymity rules

An anonymised case study must not reveal an employer through indirect clues.

Remove or generalise:

- Company and product names
- Internal programme names
- Employee names and job titles where identifiable
- Exact dates and chronology where unnecessary
- Exact technology combinations if uniquely revealing
- Exact financial figures, volumes and headcounts
- Internal terminology, field names and process labels
- Branded templates, fonts, colours and screenshots

Use descriptions such as:

- Global B2B technology company
- Multi-region enterprise
- High-growth SaaS business
- Post-merger commercial organisation
- Product-led and sales-led revenue model

## Metrics

Use exact metrics only where the figure is already public, clearly owned, or cannot identify a former employer.

Otherwise use:

- Rounded values
- Ranges
- Indexed improvements
- Directional outcomes
- Qualitative evidence with clear limitations

Never invent an outcome to improve a case study.

## Venture boundaries

### Lynr

Lynr may be named because it is an owned venture. Private financial information, customer information and commercially sensitive delivery details remain excluded unless explicitly approved.

### ThinkBud

ThinkBud may be represented through a high-level product-building case study.

The portfolio process must not:

- Modify the ThinkBud repository
- Copy ThinkBud source code into this organisation
- Copy production configuration or secrets
- Expose child, parent or customer data
- Reproduce proprietary implementation details unnecessarily

### Other ventures

A venture may be named only where Christopher owns or controls the relevant intellectual property and the material has passed the review checklist.

## Recipient expectations

This repository is private and shared selectively. Access does not grant permission to redistribute, publish or reuse the material.

## Required review

Before any new file is committed:

1. Confirm ownership or permitted use.
2. Remove identifying employer and customer information.
3. Replace real data with synthetic data.
4. Check hidden metadata and embedded links.
5. Run the repository review checklist.
6. Run secret and personal-data scans.
7. Record the source treatment in `SOURCE-PROVENANCE.md`.
