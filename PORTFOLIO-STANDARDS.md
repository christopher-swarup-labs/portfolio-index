# Portfolio Standards

## Purpose

Every repository should make Christopher Swarup's judgement, systems thinking and execution capability easier to understand.

The standard is not "more content". The standard is clear evidence.

## Portfolio principles

### 1. Show the operating problem

Start with the commercial or organisational issue, not the tool.

Good:

> Pipeline numbers changed depending on who produced the report.

Weak:

> Implemented a CRM dashboard.

### 2. Make the reasoning visible

Explain:

- What was happening
- Why it mattered commercially
- What constraints existed
- Which design choices were made
- Which trade-offs were accepted
- How adoption and governance were handled

### 3. Separate evidence from inference

Clearly distinguish:

- Verified outcomes
- Directional signals
- Professional judgement
- Hypotheses still to be tested

### 4. Build reusable models

Turn individual experience into company-neutral frameworks, decision rules, templates and diagnostics.

### 5. Prefer practical substance

A small working validator with synthetic data is stronger than a large collection of generic slides.

## Standard case-study structure

Each case study should follow this structure:

```text
1. Executive summary
2. Business situation
3. Symptoms and operating diagnosis
4. Constraints
5. Design principles
6. Target operating model
7. Process, system and data decisions
8. Implementation sequence
9. Governance and adoption
10. Measurement approach
11. Outcomes
12. Lessons and what I would change
```

## Evidence types

Rank evidence in this order:

1. Working tool or reproducible demonstration
2. Synthetic dataset with documented logic
3. Operating model with decision rules
4. Anonymised case study with grounded outcomes
5. Diagram or framework
6. Narrative claim without supporting artefact

Aim to move important claims higher up this ladder.

## Writing standard

- Human and direct
- Commercially grounded
- Clear enough for an executive to scan
- Detailed enough for an operator to inspect
- Free from hype and vague transformation language
- Honest about uncertainty and limitations

## Visual standard

- Clean consulting-style diagrams
- White or pale background
- Charcoal typography
- Light structural lines
- One restrained accent colour
- No decorative stock imagery
- No copied corporate templates

## Metrics standard

Where relevant, include measures such as:

- Conversion rate
- Stage velocity
- SLA compliance
- Routing accuracy
- Forecast variance
- Data completeness
- Tool adoption
- Campaign cycle time
- Cost to serve
- Pipeline coverage
- Retention or expansion signal quality

Metrics must be defined, not merely named.

## Repository standard

Each major repository should contain:

```text
README.md
/docs
/examples
/diagrams
/tests or validation logic where relevant
SECURITY.md
SOURCE-PROVENANCE.md
```

## Definition of done

A portfolio item is complete when:

- The commercial problem is clear
- The operating logic is understandable
- The artefact is independently created
- No restricted information is present
- Metrics and claims are supportable
- The item has passed the review checklist
- A reviewer can understand Christopher's contribution without employer context
