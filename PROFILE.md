## Executive summary

I design and build the operating systems behind predictable B2B revenue — connecting commercial strategy, process, ownership, platforms, data, analytics and AI so teams can execute with clarity.

The through-line across seven companies is the same problem in different clothing: a go-to-market organisation that has outgrown the way it was wired. Leads arrive but nobody agrees what they mean. Marketing reports pipeline the CRO does not believe. Three acquired companies each brought a lifecycle model and none of them survived contact with the others. My work is to make the underlying decisions, ownership, process and data trustworthy first, then automate.

I have held Marketing Operations and Revenue Systems leadership across enterprise infrastructure (Equinix, Nutanix), high-growth European SaaS (Pleo), a multi-brand digital experience platform formed by merger (Contentsquare, following the Hotjar and Heap acquisitions), and an AI-native B2B software company (DevRev). Scope has consistently included direct team leadership, responsibility for substantial operations and technology budgets, Tier-1 martech ownership, and executive partnership with CRO, CMO and Revenue Operations leadership.

---

## Operating principle

> Build the operating foundation first. Add automation and AI only when the underlying decisions, ownership, process and data can be trusted.

This is not conservatism about AI. It is a claim about sequence. An AI layer applied to a GTM system with contested lifecycle definitions and unreliable CRM data does not produce speed — it produces confident, well-formatted, wrong answers at scale, and it destroys the credibility of the operations function that shipped it.

## The problems I am best suited to solve

- A GTM organisation where marketing-reported pipeline and sales-believed pipeline have diverged, and nobody can reconcile them.
- A post-merger or post-acquisition estate with two or more competing lifecycle models, CRM instances or attribution approaches.
- A martech estate that has accreted past the point of governance — overlapping tools, unclear system authority, unowned integrations.
- A Marketing Operations function positioned as a service desk that needs to become revenue infrastructure, including the team design and executive framing that requires.
- An organisation about to apply AI to a GTM foundation that is not ready for it.

## Where I am a weaker fit

Deep product analytics ownership, sales compensation design, and finance-owned revenue accounting are adjacent to my work but not my core. I would rather name that than be discovered at month three.
