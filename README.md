## How the work will be presented

Every case study will follow the same reasoning path:

```text

      ↓
Operating diagnosis
      ↓
Constraints and trade-offs
      ↓
Design principles
      ↓
Process, system and data decisions
      ↓
Ownership and governance
      ↓
Implementation and adoption
      ↓
Measurement, limitations and lessons
```

The purpose is not to show polished historic slides. It is to show how I think, what I build, how I make change work and where the evidence has limits.

## Evidence controls

Every portfolio artefact must be:

- Independently written or built for this portfolio
- Clear about my role and contribution
- Company-neutral unless it relates to an owned venture
- Free from customer, employee, personal and confidential information
- Based on synthetic data where examples are needed
- Clear about whether it is implemented work, reconstructed experience or an independent strategic exercise
- Honest about what is verified, generalised, directional or unproven
- Useful enough that another senior operator can understand and challenge the model

## Drive audit and safety boundary

The connected Drive was reviewed as a source of professional memory and evidence. It was not treated as a file-import queue.

The audit identified substantial operator evidence as well as high-risk material such as credentials, identity and employment records, contracts, production data, confidential system material and third-party intellectual property.

No historic employer file will be uploaded directly. No source artefact will be made safe through superficial redaction.

The operating rule is:

> Rebuild the insight. Never migrate the source file.
