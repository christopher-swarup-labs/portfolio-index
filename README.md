## How the work is presented

Every major case follows a consistent reasoning path:

```text
Commercial problem
      ↓
Operating diagnosis
      ↓
Constraints and trade-offs
      ↓
Design principles
      ↓
Process, system and data decisions
      ↓
Ownership and governance
      ↓
Implementation and adoption
      ↓
Measurement, limitations and lessons
```

The purpose is not to show polished historic slides. It is to make the thinking, operating logic, implementation choices, accountability and evidence quality visible.

## Evidence labels

Every major item must be labelled as one of:

- **Implemented experience — independently reconstructed**
- **Original framework**
- **Synthetic demonstration**
- **Independent strategic exercise**
- **Venture retrospective**
- **Reference only**

This prevents hypothetical work from being presented as implementation and distinguishes original thinking from external learning.

## Evidence controls

Every portfolio artefact must be:

- Independently written or built for this portfolio
- Clear about my role and contribution
- Company-neutral unless it relates to an owned venture
- Free from customer, employee, personal and confidential information
- Based on synthetic data where examples are needed
- Honest about what is verified, generalised, directional or unproven
- Useful enough that a senior operator, executive or investor can understand and challenge it
