## How to review the work

The portfolio will be organised around a simple sequence:

```text
Commercial problem
      ↓
Operating diagnosis
      ↓
Design principles
      ↓
Process, system and data decisions
      ↓
Implementation and governance
      ↓
Measurement and lessons
```

Each case study will make the reasoning visible. The goal is not to show polished slides. It is to show how I think, what I build and how I make change work in practice.

## Evidence standards

Every item added here must be:

- Independently written for this portfolio
- Company-neutral unless it relates to a venture I own
- Free from personal, customer, employee or confidential information
- Based on synthetic data where examples are needed
- Clear about what was built, what was learned and what remains unproven
- Useful enough that another operator could understand the model

## Confidentiality boundary

No historic employer file will be uploaded directly.

This repository must not contain:

- Employer or customer names in anonymised case studies
- Internal presentations, spreadsheets, screenshots or system exports
- Production CRM, marketing, financial or customer data
- Credentials, secrets, tokens, private links or recovery codes
- Personal information about employees, customers or family members
- Proprietary source code copied from another repository

ThinkBud may be represented as a high-level product-building case study, but its source repository, production configuration and proprietary implementation are outside this portfolio workflow.

See [CONFIDENTIALITY.md](CONFIDENTIALITY.md), [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and [PORTFOLIO-STANDARDS.md](PORTFOLIO-STANDARDS.md) before adding material.
