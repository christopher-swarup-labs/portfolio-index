# Reviewer Guide

You should not need to read the whole repository. Start with the route closest to the decision you are trying to make.

## Senior role / hiring decision

1. [Employer Brief](EMPLOYER-BRIEF.md) — current status, role fit and the problems I can own
2. [Professional Profile](PROFILE.md) — career chronology, leadership scope and quantified outcomes
3. [Revenue Operating System case](case-studies/flagship/revenue-operating-system.md) — broad GTM diagnosis, design and governance
4. [Unified Lifecycle Governance](case-studies/flagship/unified-lifecycle-governance.md) — definitions, ownership, routing, handoffs and adoption
5. [Modern MOPS Team Operating Model](case-studies/leadership/modern-mops-team-operating-model.md) — team design, priorities and distributed delivery
6. [Frameworks](frameworks/README.md) and [Runnable Tools](tools/README.md) — whether the thinking can be taken into practical detail

By the end, you should have a clear view of whether I can:

- Diagnose why a GTM operating model is underperforming
- Connect strategy, ownership, process, systems, data and reporting
- Move from executive framing into implementation detail
- Lead across functions with different incentives
- Build Marketing or Revenue Operations as commercial infrastructure
- Apply AI without weakening accountability or decision quality

## How work is labelled

You will see explicit labels such as:

- **Composite implemented experience — independently reconstructed**
- **Original portfolio framework**
- **Portfolio-safe skill specification**
- **Synthetic demonstration**
- **Independent strategic exercise**
- **Owned venture build**
- **Concept under development**

Judge the portfolio by the quality of diagnosis, trade-offs, implementation thinking, evidence boundaries and practical usability — not by the number of files.
