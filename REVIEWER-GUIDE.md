# Reviewer Guide

This repository is designed for two decision-making audiences: potential employers evaluating senior operating capability, and investors or advisers evaluating judgement, repeatability and founder potential.

It is not a CV archive and it is not a collection of historic employer files. It is a structured evidence room showing how Christopher diagnoses operating problems, designs systems, leads change and turns expertise into reusable intellectual property.

## Employer review path — 15 to 25 minutes

Use this path when assessing Christopher for senior Marketing Operations, Revenue Operations, GTM Strategy, Transformation or Operating-System leadership roles.

1. Start with [README.md](README.md) for positioning and capability coverage.
2. Review the [Workstream Coverage Map](WORKSTREAM-COVERAGE.md) to understand breadth and depth.
3. Review the first flagship operator cases as they are completed:
   - Revenue Operating System
   - Unified Lifecycle Governance
   - Campaign Operations OS
   - Pipeline Truth and Attribution
   - Modern MOPS Team Operating Model
4. Review [Skills and Systems](SKILLS-AND-SYSTEMS.md) for AI-assisted workflows, reusable operating frameworks and decision-support architecture.
5. Review the [Case Study Index](CASE-STUDY-INDEX.md) for the complete build queue.
6. Review the evidence controls only when diligence requires them:
   - [Source Provenance](SOURCE-PROVENANCE.md)
   - [Portfolio Standards](PORTFOLIO-STANDARDS.md)
   - [Confidentiality Policy](CONFIDENTIALITY.md)

### What an employer should be able to determine

- Can Christopher diagnose why a GTM operating model is underperforming?
- Can he connect strategy, process, systems, data, analytics and ownership?
- Can he lead across Marketing, Sales, SDR, Partnerships, Customer Success, Finance, Data and Technology?
- Can he design operating rules that survive beyond one tool or one person?
- Can he translate executive priorities into implementation, governance and adoption?
- Can he use AI without weakening accountability or decision quality?

## Investor and adviser review path — 15 to 25 minutes

Use this path when evaluating Christopher as a founder, operator-founder, adviser or builder of repeatable services and products.

1. Start with [README.md](README.md) for the operator thesis.
2. Review the flagship operating frameworks to see whether the expertise is structured and repeatable.
3. Review [Skills and Systems](SKILLS-AND-SYSTEMS.md) to assess whether operating knowledge has been converted into reusable workflows and decision products.
4. Review the first completed venture retrospective when available.
5. Review the [Roadmap](ROADMAP.md) to understand sequencing, discipline and the boundary between evidence and aspiration.
6. Review the [Exclusion Register](EXCLUSION-REGISTER.md) to understand the risk and confidentiality posture.

### What an investor or adviser should be able to determine

- Does Christopher have repeatable operator insight rather than isolated experience?
- Can he identify commercially valuable problems and convert them into offers, tools or products?
- Can he sequence foundation, execution, automation and AI sensibly?
- Can he build with appropriate governance, risk controls and handback?
- Is he clear about what has been implemented, reconstructed, tested or remains unproven?

## Evidence labels

Every material artefact should carry one of the following labels:

- **Implemented experience** — based on work Christopher personally led or materially contributed to, independently reconstructed for the portfolio.
- **Original framework** — created specifically for this portfolio from accumulated professional judgement.
- **Synthetic demonstration** — runnable or visual proof using fictional organisations and generated data.
- **Independent strategic exercise** — a role, interview or market assessment that demonstrates judgement but was not implemented.
- **Venture retrospective** — a founder-led concept or business, with current state and limitations stated clearly.
- **Reference only** — third-party learning or public research that is not presented as Christopher's original work.

## What is deliberately absent

This repository does not contain personal contact details, family information, contracts, payslips, identity records, credentials, recovery codes, customer data, employee data, production exports, internal screenshots, private URLs or copied employer documents.

## Review principle

> Assess the quality of the diagnosis, the operating logic, the trade-offs, the implementation thinking and the honesty of the evidence—not the volume of files.
