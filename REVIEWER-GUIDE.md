# Reviewer Guide

You should not need to read the whole repository. Start with the route closest to the decision you are trying to make.

## Senior role / hiring decision

1. [Employer Brief](EMPLOYER-BRIEF.md) — current status, role fit, leadership scale and the problems I can own
2. [Professional Profile](PROFILE.md) — career chronology, quantified outcomes and AI/GTM capability
3. [Revenue Operating System case](case-studies/flagship/revenue-operating-system.md) — broad GTM diagnosis, design and governance
4. [Unified Lifecycle Governance](case-studies/flagship/unified-lifecycle-governance.md) — definitions, ownership, routing, handoffs and adoption
5. [Modern MOPS Team Operating Model](case-studies/leadership/modern-mops-team-operating-model.md) — team design, priorities and distributed delivery
6. [AI GTM Operations](AI-GTM-OPS.md) and [Runnable Tools](tools/README.md) — whether the operating logic can be taken into controlled automation

By the end, you should have a clear view of whether I can:

- Diagnose why a GTM operating model is underperforming
- Connect strategy, ownership, process, systems, data and reporting
- Move from executive framing into implementation detail
- Lead across functions with different incentives
- Build Marketing or Revenue Operations as commercial infrastructure
- Translate operating logic into AI-assisted workflows without weakening accountability
- Make the work testable, governed and usable after the initial transformation

## AI / GTM Systems reviewer

Start with:

1. [AI GTM Operations](AI-GTM-OPS.md) — the operating model for context, routing, evidence, controls and learning
2. [GTM Command Center](skills/command-center/README.md) — orchestration and control-plane architecture
3. [GTM Ops Decision Router](tools/gtm-ops-router/README.md) — coordination layer, evidence gates, ambiguity handling and unit tests
4. [Pipeline Quality Scanner](tools/pipeline-quality-scanner/README.md) — pipeline data-quality rules expressed in code
5. [Lifecycle Transition Validator](tools/lifecycle-validator/README.md) — lifecycle policy represented as explicit rules
6. [Runnable Tools](tools/README.md) — CI-backed synthetic proof
7. [Security Policy](SECURITY.md) and [Confidentiality & Evidence Standard](CONFIDENTIALITY.md) — operating and publication boundaries

The questions I would ask are simple:

- Is the commercial decision clear?
- Is trusted context separated from live operational data?
- Are the routing and decision rules inspectable?
- Does missing evidence reduce automation rather than increase confidence?
- Are tests and failure modes visible?
- Is material action gated by a named human owner?
- Are changes to context and logic versioned?
- Can the system remember outcomes without silently rewriting policy?

## How work is labelled

You will see explicit labels such as:

- **Composite implemented experience — independently reconstructed**
- **Original portfolio framework**
- **Portfolio-safe architecture reconstruction**
- **Portfolio-safe skill specification**
- **Synthetic demonstration**
- **Independent strategic exercise**
- **Owned venture build**
- **Concept under development**

Judge the portfolio by the quality of diagnosis, trade-offs, implementation thinking, evidence boundaries, controls and practical usability — not by the number of files or how polished the language sounds.
