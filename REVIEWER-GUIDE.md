# Reviewer Guide

Thanks for spending time on this.

I have tried to make the repository easy to navigate because I know you are not here to read every file. You are probably trying to answer a fairly specific question about me, so choose the route that is closest to the decision you need to make.

## You are considering me for a senior operating role

Start here:

1. [Employer Brief](EMPLOYER-BRIEF.md) — where I add the most value and the problems I am equipped to own
2. [Building ThinkBud](case-studies/ventures/building-thinkbud.md) — whether I can take a complex idea from a blank page into a working, secure and tested product
3. [Professional Profile](PROFILE.md) — career scope, leadership experience and functional depth
4. [Revenue Operating System case](case-studies/flagship/revenue-operating-system.md) — how I diagnose and structure a broad GTM operating problem
5. [Unified Lifecycle Governance case](case-studies/flagship/unified-lifecycle-governance.md) — how I handle definitions, ownership, handoffs and adoption
6. [Modern MOPS Team Operating Model](case-studies/leadership/modern-mops-team-operating-model.md) — how I think about teams, priorities and distributed delivery

By the end, you should have a clear view of whether I can:

- Diagnose why an operating model is underperforming
- Connect strategy, ownership, process, systems, data and reporting
- Move from executive framing into implementation detail
- Lead across functions with different incentives
- Build Marketing or Revenue Operations as commercial infrastructure
- Use AI without weakening accountability or decision quality
- Build and stabilise a software product rather than only advise on one

- **Original portfolio framework**
- **Portfolio-safe skill specification**
- **Synthetic demonstration**
- **Independent strategic exercise**
- **Owned venture build**
- **Venture retrospective**
- **Reference only**
- **Concept under development**

## The standard I would use to judge the portfolio

Do not judge it by the number of files or by how polished the language sounds.

Judge it by the quality of the diagnosis, whether the trade-offs are visible, whether the implementation thinking is practical, whether the limits are honest and whether the operating logic would survive contact with a real organisation or a real user.
