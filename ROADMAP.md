## Standard for every new asset

Every new case, framework, skill or tool must include:

- A clear business problem
- My role or the correct evidence classification
- The diagnosis and trade-offs
- Decision rules
- Ownership and governance
- An implementation or usage sequence
- Adoption and human accountability
- Measures or evidence status
- Limitations
- Source provenance
- Confidentiality review
- A first-person voice check against [VOICE-AND-STYLE.md](VOICE-AND-STYLE.md)
