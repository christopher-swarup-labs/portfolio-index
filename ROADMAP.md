# Portfolio Build Roadmap

## Objective

Build a private operator evidence room for potential employers, investors and advisers. It must prove strategic judgement, systems design, implementation depth, transformation leadership, reusable intellectual property and venture-building potential without exposing employer, customer, personal or proprietary information.

## Audience standard

The portfolio must support two primary decisions:

### Employer decision

Can Christopher diagnose complex GTM problems, design the operating system underneath growth, lead cross-functional implementation and make the change usable?

### Investor or adviser decision

Does Christopher possess repeatable operator insight that can be converted into differentiated services, workflows, tools or products?

Every phase must strengthen one or both decisions. File volume is not a success measure.

## Delivery rule

Portfolio production proceeds collection by collection. A visually attractive or fashionable item must not displace a foundational capability that the evidence proves.

Every new file must help a potential employer, investor or adviser make a better decision about Christopher's judgement, leadership, execution capability or repeatable intellectual property.
