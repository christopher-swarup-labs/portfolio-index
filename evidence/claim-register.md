# Claim Register

This register classifies professional claims before they appear in reviewer-facing narrative.

**Rule:** a metric appearing in a CV, interview document or historic internal artefact is a self-reported single source. It is not independently verified evidence. Exact internal figures, named programmes and company-specific operating detail are not reproduced here.

## Classification key

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Public / approved professional context** | Consistent with public profile or approved professional material |
| **Approved professional material** | Present in approved CV, certificate or professional records |
| **Safely generalised** | True in substance; sensitive detail withheld or rounded |
| **Directional** | Direction and scope appear credible; exact measurement is not yet verified |
| **Needs verification** | Cannot be published as fact until source, definition and attribution are confirmed |
| **Excluded** | Removed for confidentiality, attribution or accuracy reasons |

## Organisational scale

| Claim family | Status | Treatment |
|---|---|---|
| Responsibility for material operations and technology investment | Safely generalised | Do not publish exact employer budget figures |
| Conflicting budget figures in historic source material | Needs verification — conflict | Resolve privately; do not connect the figures to a named company in reviewer material |

| Aggregate ARR across multiple employers | Excluded | Creates misleading personal attribution |

## Company and customer confidentiality controls

- Public company names may appear only in the factual career timeline.
- Named companies must not be connected to confidential metrics, customers, schemas, system configurations, internal programmes or operational incidents.
- No customer, prospect, employee, partner or supplier data may enter this register.
- No exact internal performance metric should appear without a safe-disclosure decision.
- Case studies must use neutral organisational descriptions and synthetic data.
- Independent strategic exercises must never be presented as implemented results.

## Summary

The defensible reviewer-facing position is:

> Christopher's scope, seniority, professional trajectory and breadth of operating responsibility are strongly evidenced. Quantified outcomes require separate verification, safe generalisation or exclusion.

This register is intentionally conservative. Credibility and confidentiality are more valuable than metric volume.
