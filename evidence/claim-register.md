# Claim Register

This register classifies professional claims before they appear in reviewer-facing narrative.

**Rule:** a metric appearing in a CV or interview document is a self-reported single source. It is not independently verified evidence. Exact internal figures are not reproduced here where they may be confidential or commercially sensitive.

## Classification key

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Public / approved professional context** | Consistent with public profile or approved professional material |
| **Approved professional material** | Present in approved CV, certificate or professional records |
| **Safely generalised** | True in substance; sensitive detail withheld or rounded |
| **Directional** | Direction and scope appear credible; exact measurement is not yet verified |
| **Needs verification** | Cannot be published as fact until source, definition and attribution are confirmed |
| **Excluded** | Removed for confidentiality, attribution or accuracy reasons |

## Language controls

- No aggregate company revenue claim should imply personal revenue ownership.
- No exact performance metric should appear without definition, source, period and contribution.
- No superlative should be used without independent evidence.
- Awards may be stated precisely only after the title and date are confirmed.
- Independent strategic exercises must never be presented as implemented results.

## Summary

The defensible reviewer-facing position is:

> Christopher's scope, seniority, professional trajectory and breadth of operating responsibility are strongly evidenced. Quantified outcomes require separate verification, safe generalisation or exclusion.

This register is intentionally more conservative than the source CV material. Credibility is more valuable than metric volume.
